How payment works

  • Step 1: Advance 30% Pay 30% of the total price to confirm your booking.
  • Step 2: Balance 70% Pay the remaining amount at least 7 days before travel.
  • Pay with JazzCash or EasyPaisa See payment details

Example: Hunza Standard for a family of 4 costs PKR 260,000. Advance is PKR 78,000 and the balance is PKR 182,000.

Cancellation and refunds

  • 15 days or more before travel 80% refund
  • 7 to 14 days before travel 50% refund
  • Less than 7 days before travel 25% refund
