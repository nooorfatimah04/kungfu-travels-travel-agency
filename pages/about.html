Our story

KungFu Travels started in 2020 with one simple idea: everyone should be able to see the beautiful places in Pakistan without stress. We plan the route, the stay and the transport, so you can enjoy the journey.

From the green valleys of Hunza to the cool hills of Murree and the lakes of Naran, we help families, friends and solo travelers create trips they remember.
