Booking Process

Complete these steps to confirm your trip.

  1. Select a destination and package. Browse Hunza Valley, Murree Hills or Naran and pick the package that fits your budget. Browse →
  2. Prepare your travel dates and traveler details. Decide your travel dates and keep the names and number of travelers ready. Register →
  3. Contact our booking team. Reach us by email, phone or the contact form and share your plan. Contact →
  4. Submit the required information. Send your details and pay with JazzCash or EasyPaisa. Pay now →
  5. Wait for confirmation. Our team will confirm your trip and send you the final itinerary. Login →
