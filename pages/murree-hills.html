Murree Hills

A pine-covered hill station near Islamabad, popular for short family trips and snowfall in winter.

  • Best time: April to September
  • Suggested stay: 2 to 3 days
  • Good for: Families, weekend trips

Places to visit

  • Mall Road

    The main promenade for walking, shopping and local snacks.

  • Patriata (New Murree)

    Chairlift and cable car rides over forested slopes.

  • Pindi Point

    Lookout with views toward Islamabad and Rawalpindi, with a chairlift.

  • Kashmir Point

    Viewpoint over a long stretch of hills, good for photos at golden hour.

  • Nathia Gali

    Quieter Galiyat town with forest walks and cooler air.
