Naran

The main town of the Kaghan Valley, with alpine lakes, meadows and the Kunhar River. It is the base for trips to Saif-ul-Malook Lake and Lalazar.

  • Best time: June to September
  • Suggested stay: 3 to 5 days
  • Good for: Couples, families, nature lovers

Places to visit

  • Saif-ul-Malook Lake

    Famous glacial lake below Malika Parbat. Reached by jeep, with boating available.

  • Lalazar

    High meadow with open views, popular for sunrise and camping.

  • Lulusar Lake

    Large lake on the road toward Babusar Top.

  • Dudipatsar Lake

    Remote lake reached by a hike, for travellers who enjoy trekking.

  • Babusar Top

    Mountain pass linking Kaghan with Gilgit-Baltistan, open seasonally.

  • Shogran and Siri Paye

    Green plateau and meadows reached by a jeep track from Kiwai.
