FAQs

Quick answers about booking, prices and travel. Tap a question to open it.

Booking

How do I book a tour?

Pick a destination and package, then contact our booking team by email, phone or the contact form. You can follow every step on the booking process page.

Can I change my destination?

Yes. Contact us as early as possible to request a change. The change depends on availability, and the price may change if you move to a different package.

Prices and payment

How do I pay?

You can pay with JazzCash or EasyPaisa. Pay 30% as an advance to confirm your booking, and the remaining balance at least 7 days before travel. Payment details are on the booking page.

Can I get a refund if I cancel?

Yes. Cancel 15 days or more before travel for an 80% refund, 7 to 14 days before for 50%, and less than 7 days before for 25%.

Travel

What is not included?

Entry tickets, lunch and personal expenses are not included. Peak season may add 10 to 15% to the price.
