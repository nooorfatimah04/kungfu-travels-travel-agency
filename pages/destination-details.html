Hunza Valley

A mountain valley on the Karakoram Highway, known for apricot orchards, old forts and views of Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar.

  • Best time: April to October
  • Suggested stay: 5 to 7 days
  • Good for: Couples, families, photography

Places to visit

  • Baltit Fort

    Centuries-old hilltop fort in Karimabad with sweeping views over the valley.

  • Altit Fort

    One of the oldest forts in Hunza, set above terraced fields and a deep gorge.

  • Attabad Lake

    Turquoise lake formed by a landslide. Boat rides are the main activity.

  • Passu Cones

    Jagged peaks near Passu village, best seen at sunrise or sunset.

  • Eagle's Nest

    Popular viewpoint above Duikar for sunset over the whole valley.

  • Khunjerab Pass

    High mountain border pass on the Karakoram Highway, open in the warmer months.
